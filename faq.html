Standard is a print on the front only. Retro is a print on both front and back, so you can upload two designs. T-shirts and hoodies: Standard 35 DT, Retro 45 DT.
A PNG with transparent background gives the cleanest result. JPG, WEBP and SVG also work. For sharp prints use an image of at least 2000 px on the longest side. When you place your order, a link to your design is added to the WhatsApp message so we print from the full-quality file.
The preview is a guide to placement and colour. Our team checks every design and may contact you on WhatsApp if the image needs adjusting before printing.
T-shirts and hoodies come in S to XXL and 8 colours. Beanies, phone cases and tote bags come in a selection of colours shown on the product page.
